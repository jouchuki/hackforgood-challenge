# Next Step

## Punchline

Prevent harm at internet scale. Turn moments of risk into pathways to help—and evidence for better prevention.

## Description

**Next Step helps platforms turn moments of risk into opportunities for prevention, support and research.**

When an existing moderation system flags a risky submission, Next Step opens an intervention that encourages the person to pause, understand the harm and find support. Platforms can start with a calm, nonjudgmental message, introduce relevant legal information and offer a clear route to help.

We built three connected experiences:

**For moderators: fast installation and a visual message builder.** Moderators can choose from six distinct starting points: a plain legal information page, a calming journey, a reflection on life and values, a factual harm brief, a compact help page or a guided sequence with optional motion. They can edit English and Dutch content, rearrange blocks, preview both languages and publish a version that stays consistent until they update it.

**For researchers: a practical way to test what helps.** Researchers can build message variants and compare them across platforms whose moderators explicitly opt into a study. Eligible intervention views are automatically randomized between variants. The research dashboard compares help-link clicks, dismissals, time spent, scrolling, content completion and movement between intervention stages. Researchers can filter results, save analysis views and export aggregate data, including through an optional PostHog connection.

**For visitors: a clear, private route to support.** The intervention runs in the browser and offers language and country selection, relevant information and direct links to support resources. English and Dutch are complete. A separate help directory works without JavaScript.

Privacy shapes the measurement system. Submitted searches and messages stay with the platform. Next Step collects bounded counters rather than visitor profiles or browsing histories, and small result cells are suppressed. Researchers can compare outcomes without receiving the original submissions or recordings of visitors’ screens.

The demo includes four runnable installation paths: a JavaScript embed, a React wrapper, an installable WordPress plugin and a real Discord bot tested in a private server. A further 19 platform-specific guides cover frameworks, forum software and Tor/onion deployment.

Next Step supplies the intervention and research tools; the host platform supplies the moderation decision. The demo uses synthetic triggers so the complete flow can be demonstrated safely.

**Our goal is to make prevention easy to deploy—and help researchers discover which messages actually encourage people to seek help.**
